# Haze demo: YouTube clip shortlist

Working notes for the `hazy-god-eye-view` fork. Collected 2026-10-24 from
YouTube search; views are as of that day.

Every clip below returned oEmbed 200 and `playableInEmbed: true`, so it plays
in the scene's `youtube-nocookie` card. **Clips were chosen from titles,
channels and dates only: nobody has watched them yet.** Before a clip goes in
a shot, watch it, note the seconds of usable raw footage (for `start`/`end`),
and check it shows what the caption claims.

Rules of thumb: prefer broadcasters and wire services (AP Archive, Reuters,
KompasTV, CNA, The Star) for raw-looking footage; avoid channels with
sensational titles for anything presented as fact; show private individuals'
faces only in news footage.
